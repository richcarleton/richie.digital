About the Game

Step into the helmet of Mikey, a tough-looking softy navigating the serene yet sometimes suffocating suburbs of the Quebec Laurentians. On his overpowered e-bike, Mikey seeks solace and escape, finding peace in the region's sprawling bike paths, tranquil parks, and secluded beaches. But a quiet, unsettling mystery begins to stir beneath the surface, challenging his perception of reality and testing his resilience.

As Mikey adjusts to new psychotropic meds, his senses are both heightened and clouded, making him an unlikely investigator in a world where the beautiful can quickly become menacing. What starts as a series of strange occurrences soon escalates into a chilling enigma, forcing Mikey to confront not only external threats but also the turbulent landscape of his own mind.

Key Features

  • First-Person E-Bike Immersion: Experience the Laurentian landscape directly from Mikey's perspective, feeling the rush of wind, the hum of the motor, and the immediate thrill of exploration.
  • A Unique Protagonist: Guide Mikey through moments of brooding introspection and boisterous defiance. Navigate his "angy" moods and deep sensitivity as he unravels a captivating mystery.
  • Laurentian World: Explore a beautifully rendered environment of bike paths, dense forests, tranquil lakeshores, and suburban streets, each holding secrets waiting to be uncovered.
  • Mystery with a Twist: What begins as a fun, intriguing puzzle gradually escalates into a genuinely scary psychological thriller. The line between reality and Mikey's internal struggles blurs.
