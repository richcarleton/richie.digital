SIGPAD // RIBBON

no pad — the strip below plays with mouse or finger.
—

X = pitch, Y = filter (top bright, bottom dark). Pen down plays, lift releases. Chord: the pen drags a triad — diatonic picks major/minor/dim from the scale as you move. Drone (D key): root + fifth under the strip (floored at C2), held until you toggle it off — solo over it. Calibrate: hit it, then sweep the pen into all four corners. Glide 0 + a scale = keyboard; scale off + glide = theremin-on-a-stick.
